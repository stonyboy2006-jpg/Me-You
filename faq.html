Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What time should I arrive?
We recommend arriving 30 minutes before the ceremony begins to find your seat and settle in.
What is the dress code?
The dress code is formal/black tie. We encourage our guests to dress in their finest attire.
Can I bring a plus one?
Please check your invitation for plus one details. If you have questions, please contact us directly.
Will there be parking?
Yes, there will be complimentary parking available at the venue.
Is there a hotel block?
We have reserved a block of rooms at a nearby hotel. Details will be shared with invited guests.
